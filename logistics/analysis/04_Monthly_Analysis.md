# Monthly Analysis

## Monthly Shipping Volume & Transportation Cost Trend

![Monthly Volume & Transportation Cost Trend](../screenshots/04_Pivot_Monthly_Volume_Transportation_Cost_Trend.png)

### Key Findings

- Monthly shipment volume was generally stable throughout 2023, with most months falling within a similar range. February had the lowest activity (**139 shipments**), while August showed the highest volume (**182 shipments**), indicating a temporary increase in operational demand rather than a significant long-term growth trend.
- **August** recorded both the highest shipment volume (**182**) and the highest total shipping cost (**$44.5K**), indicating a peak in transportation activity.
- Shipping costs generally increased during the second half of the year, with another noticeable peak in **November ($40.4K)** despite handling fewer shipments than August.

---

## Average Shipping Cost Trend

![Average Shipping Cost Trend](../screenshots/04_Pivot_Average_Shipping_Cost_Trend.png)

### Key Findings

- **August** experienced the highest average shipping cost (**$254.13**) even though the average shipping distance (**1,237 miles**) was lower than in several other months. This suggests that higher costs were not driven solely by longer routes.
- **November** combined one of the highest average shipping costs (**$230.85**) with the longest average transit time (**4.49 days**), indicating a period of lower operational efficiency.
- **December** presents an interesting contrast: shipments traveled the shortest average distance (**1,184 miles**) and had the fastest transit time (**3.88 days**), yet the average shipping cost remained relatively high (**$212.14**). This may indicate changes in carrier pricing, shipment characteristics, or seasonal demand.

---

## Delivery Performance Trend

![Delivery Performance Trend](../screenshots/04_Pivot_Delivery_Performance_Trend.png)

### Key Findings

- Delivery performance was generally stable throughout most of the year, with delivered on-time shipments remaining above **81%** in nearly every month.
- **October** achieved the highest delivery completion rate (**86.13%**) while maintaining one of the lowest in-transit rates (**0.58%**), suggesting efficient shipment completion.
- **December** showed the weakest delivery performance, with the lowest delivery rate (**77.65%**) and the highest delay rate (**14.12%**). This pattern may reflect seasonal shipping pressure during the holiday period.
- Lost shipment rates remained relatively low overall but increased during **November (3.37%)** and **December (2.94%)**, making these months worth further investigation.

---

## Transportation Efficiency Trend

![Transportation Efficiency Trend](../screenshots/04_Pivot_Transportation_Efficiency_Trend.png)

### Key Findings

- Transportation distance remained relatively consistent throughout the year, with most months ranging between **200K and 236K miles**.
- **May** recorded the highest transportation distance (**236K miles**), while **August** showed the highest average cost per mile (**$0.20**), indicating that increased transportation costs were not driven solely by distance.
- **November** combined a higher cost per mile (**$0.18**) with the longest average transit time (**4.49 days**), suggesting reduced transportation efficiency during that period.

---

# Overall Insights and Recommendations
- **Transportation costs increased during specific periods despite relatively stable shipment volume.** August had the highest operational activity (**182 shipments**) and the highest transportation cost (**$44.5K**), while November also showed elevated costs (**$40.4K**) with fewer shipments. This indicates that cost changes were influenced not only by shipment volume but also by factors such as carrier pricing, shipment characteristics, or transportation efficiency.
- **August and November require further cost review.** August recorded the highest average shipping cost (**$254.13**) despite a lower average distance (**1,237 miles**), while November combined a high average cost (**$230.85**) with the longest average transit time (**4.49 days**). These periods should be analyzed further by carrier and route to identify the main cost drivers.
- **Delivery performance declined at the end of the year.** October achieved the strongest performance with **86.13% of shipments delivered on time**, while December showed the weakest results with **77.65% delivered on time** and the highest delay rate (**14.12%**). Seasonal demand and carrier capacity should be evaluated as possible contributors.
- **November showed signs of reduced transportation efficiency.** While most months maintained similar transportation distance and cost per mile levels, November combined a higher cost per mile (**$0.18**) with slower delivery (**4.49 days**), indicating a potential increase in transportation inefficiency.
- **Recommended next step:** Analyze carrier and route performance by month to determine whether higher costs and delivery delays are concentrated among specific carriers, warehouses, or shipping lanes.
